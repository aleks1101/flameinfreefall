THE PROJECT

Why study fire in space?

Spacecraft are closed environments. Understanding fire in microgravity is crucial to protect crews and design safer missions.

Relevance

Without ordinary buoyancy-driven convection, flames and smoke behave differently. Ventilation, oxygen availability and materials influence fire risks inside spacecraft.

Project goal

Explain microgravity combustion and help students explore the scientific factors that influence spacecraft fire safety.

Research tasks

  • Review NASA combustion experiments.
  • Compare Earth gravity and microgravity conceptually.
  • Build an interactive educational simulator.
  • Communicate limitations and research sources.

Method and limitations

This site combines NASA public research summaries with qualitative educational visualizations. Simulated results are not measured NASA data and must not be used for engineering safety decisions.
