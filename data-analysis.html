Scientific Interpretation

Microgravity reduces buoyancy-driven convection. Diffusion and forced airflow therefore play important roles in flame behavior. Gas measurements can help investigate combustion, but interpretation requires experiment-specific conditions and verified records.

Spacecraft Fire Safety

Research into ignition, flame spread and extinguishment helps scientists improve spacecraft fire protection.
