FLEX

Flame Extinguishment Experiment

FLEX investigates the combustion of liquid fuel droplets in microgravity and explores flame extinction under different conditions.

BASS-II

Burning and Suppression of Solids

BASS-II investigates combustion and flame spread over solid materials in microgravity, including the influence of ventilation.

Scientific Analysis

In microgravity, buoyancy-driven convection is reduced. Diffusion and forced airflow become especially important in determining flame shape and combustion behavior.

Spacecraft Fire Safety

Understanding ignition, flame spread and extinguishment helps researchers develop safer spacecraft materials and fire protection strategies.
